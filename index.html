The Aurevio approach

Good design is mostly knowing what to leave out.

I’m an independent frontend developer focused on clean, responsive websites that feel considered instead of assembled from a template.

That means strong hierarchy, deliberate spacing, useful motion and layouts that survive the trip from a big monitor to a very unforgiving phone screen.
