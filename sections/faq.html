FAQ

The boring questions.
Answered.

Clear expectations before a project starts. If your project is unusual, just ask.

01How much does a website cost?+
Pricing depends on scope, pages, content and interaction. Aurevio scopes the actual work instead of forcing every project into the same price.
02How long does a project take?+
A focused landing page is faster than a multi-page business site. The timeline is agreed after the pages, content and revision scope are clear.
03Can you redesign my existing website?+
Yes. A redesign can focus on visual direction, structure, mobile usability, calls to action or a full frontend rebuild.
04What do I need to provide?+
Usually: business details, logo if you have one, photos or other assets, the pages you need and the action you want visitors to take.
05Do you build online stores or complex apps?+
Aurevio currently focuses on frontend websites and smaller web experiences. Ecommerce, authentication, databases and larger backend systems are scoped separately rather than overpromised.
